Run sheets first, the rest when you need it

Use a prepared run sheet, check access and rehearse. The pre-survey and the playbook help you adapt it when needed.

Planning in short
  • Choose a prepared 30, 60 or 90 minute session. Its run sheet explains the slides, exercises and timing.
  • The tools below adapt a session to a specific group. They are optional.

← Trainer hub

For an ordinary session, start with the prepared run sheet. It already names the slides, the exercise, the expected output, the check and the cut rule. The material further down is for adapting a session to a specific group, and none of it is needed for your first run.

Optional: adapt a session to your group

Use these when the prepared sheet does not fit: a group with very different experience levels, a market with its own priorities, or a second session for people who already ran one. Change how you explain the task, how you pair people and how you discuss applying it at work; keep the task, the check and the minutes as the run sheet has them.
