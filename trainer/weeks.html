Your first four weeks

Four small moves on material that already exists. No launch event needed.

Not sure where to start? Take the 30-second version.

← Trainer hub

You do not need a big launch. You need four small moves in four weeks, each on material that already exists. Everything linked here sits on the start page.

Before week one: know your room

  • Copy the pre-survey once, then send your copy's link to your sales team. Five minutes for them; the answers belong to you.
  • Smoke-test the platform where you will show it: karcher-ai-sales.web.app loads, the tour plays, one card opens.
  • Book two slots now: fifteen minutes in an existing team meeting (week one), and sixty minutes for a real session (week two or three).

Week one: the fifteen-minute first look

Do not start with a training. Start inside a meeting that already exists, with the first-look deck: one live card, one small exercise, done. It is the safest first run, and it creates demand for the real session.

  • Run the first-look deck (fifteen minutes, script in the notes).
  • Close with one commitment per person: one card they will try this week.
  • Put the platform link in the meeting notes; it stays there every week from now on.

Week two: read your survey, plan the real session

By now the answers are in. Let them decide the content, not your taste.

  • Open your response sheet, attach it to the Session-Planner Gem, and tell it your session length. It drafts the plan, the opening quotes, and the skip list; you stay the judge.
  • Pick the size honestly: sixty minutes runs the Basics session as it is; ninety adds the group's top cards hands-on; two hours adds sales-cycle steps on the practice case.
  • Check accounts: every participant can sign in to Workspace with Gemini. No account, no hands-on.

Week three: the first real session

  • Run the session deck for your size, script in the notes, exercises on the slides.
  • Protect the hands-on minutes; they matter more than finishing your slides.
  • End every exercise with the handoff: "now run the same recipe on one of your own accounts". That minute turns a demo into a habit.
  • Close with commitments again: one card or step per person, and one visible sign it worked.

Week four: make it a rhythm, and say so

  • In the team meeting, ask the one question: "what did you try, what did it save?"
  • Name the card of the week; one use case, one habit.
  • Send your first progress note to the Academy: three numbers, one win, one blocker. Ten minutes.
  • Set a checkpoint about thirty days out: "what did you actually use?"

If leadership will not give you the slots, that has its own page. If energy dips after week four, that is normal, and fixable.
