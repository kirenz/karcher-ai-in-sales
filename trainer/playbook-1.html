One session cannot be optimal for a Key Account Manager and a Sales Director at the same time. The pre-survey sorts the room; the agenda follows from it.

The clusters and their core

Cluster A — Key Account & Field Sales. KAMs, direct reps, business development. Complex consultative selling, several stakeholders, individual offers. Core: the sales-cycle course on NorthStar, with full lesson depth (idea → recipe → example Doc) on research (step 2), needs analysis (step 5), and value argumentation (step 6).

Cluster B — Sales Leadership & Management. Directors, area and national sales managers, center managers. Steering pipeline, coaching, strategy, reporting. Core: the Your-data cards run on the practice tracker, step 9 (follow-up & steering), the leadership chapters in the guide, and the team routine they will install.

Mixed room. The course core plus whatever scores highest in the grid: a shared opening (tour, data-safety Basic, one Your-data card), then course steps for the sellers and step 9 plus the leadership chapters for the leaders. Assign the cluster by the work people describe, not by title; an Online Director lands in B.

The four reading rules

What you read in the results What it decides
The focus question (sell yourself, or steer a team) Cluster A or B, and therefore the core above
AI experience (Beginner / Basic / Intermediate / Advanced) How much foundations time. A Beginner/Basic majority means guided tour first, then the prompting and data-safety Basics
The usefulness grid (rows 1–6 are the Quick-Help groups, row 7 is the course) Which doors you open; the top rows name it literally. Reference pattern from the original survey across 75 colleagues: data & pipeline first, then meeting prep & research, then proposals & content
Expectations and concerns (free text) Your opening: quote two of the “one thing I want” answers; the data-safety Basic answers most of the concerns; someone who already uses AI can show it

Aim for responses from at least half the group, read them in the Forms summary view.

The three instruments

Make your own copy of the pre-survey and send your copy’s link, so the responses land in your own Sheet. The Session-Planner Gem drafts the plan from that Sheet; the Trainer Notebook answers questions about the materials with citations. To try the Gem before you have a group, copy an example result sheet (seller group, leader group; synthetic answers, no real people) and attach it.

Worked example: two groups, two agendas

Group A — twelve Key Account Managers, mostly Intermediate, top needs research and proposals. Multi-stakeholder consultative selling, so they get a course sprint:

Tour (2 min) → data-safety Basic → step 2 Research (heavy, with the live ISS demo) → step 3 First contact → step 5 Needs analysis → step 6 Value argument → step 7 Presentation & demo → step 9 Follow-up → commitments. ~4 hours. Accent: full lesson depth on steps 2, 5, and 6; the translation card as a side bite.

Group B — ten Area Sales Managers and Center Managers, top needs pipeline and reporting. They steer teams rather than run individual deals, so the agenda tilts to oversight and enablement:

Tour (2 min) → See your pipeline at a glance (everyone runs it on the practice tracker) → Turn your sheet into a short report → step 9 Follow-up & steering → the leadership chapters in the guide (pipeline steering, coaching) → how to run “card of the week” with their own teams → commitments. ~3 hours.
