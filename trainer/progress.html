Show your progress

Three numbers, one win, one blocker. Ten minutes a month.

What should you report? Thirty seconds.

← Trainer hub

Progress that nobody sees does not exist, for your leadership or for the Academy. Keep the tracking so light that it survives a busy month: count three things, collect one story, send one short note.

Count what is countable

  • Sessions held, and how many people sat in them.
  • Which cards or steps you ran; that is the content story.
  • Survey answers: run the pre-survey again after about eight weeks and compare self-rated skill and the top asks. That before/after is your strongest number.

Collect one win per session

One artifact or one sentence, anonymized: a pipeline pivot someone built on their real sheet, a follow-up drafted in five minutes, a quote like "the proposal outline took ten minutes instead of an evening". One is enough; it is the part of your note people remember.

Log the session while it is fresh

One entry per session, about a minute: market, format, participants, the win, and a blocker if you hit one. It lands in one shared list, so the Academy sees what is happening across the markets without anyone chasing reports, and your monthly note writes itself from your own entries.

The monthly note to the Academy

One short mail a month, same shape every time. Copy the template:

AI in Sales rollout — [market], [month] Sessions: [n] sessions, [n] participants Content: [cards / steps you ran] Win of the month: [one sentence or one artifact] Blocker: [one sentence, or "none"] Next month: [one planned move]

Send it to your Kärcher Sales Enablement contact. The blocker line matters most: that is how the Academy learns where trainers need backup, and it is where a "no" from a pushback situation belongs.
