The checks

Each exercise ends with one check against the source. That is the habit the workshop teaches; protect these minutes.

  • Pipeline table, slide 12. Everyone adds up one small segment in the rows and compares it with the table. Said to a partner: “I checked these rows; my total was …; the table matched / differed by ….” If it differs, look at which rows were included and correct it.
  • Report, slides 15–16. Take the statements one at a time: one stage total, the largest open deal against the value column, the reference date behind any claim about recent contact. Correct what the rows do not support.

If something does not work

  • The table shows only in the side panel. Ask again for the table on a new tab called pipeline value. If a device still fails, pair that person with a colleague who has the table on screen.
  • The report comes back as a table. Ask again for plain sentences.
  • A copy fails, or Gemini is missing on a device. Pair up at a working device; the check works in pairs too.

If time runs short

Shorten the five-minute pair round first. If more is needed, drop the report block (slides 13–16) as a unit and finish the table and its check. Keep the own-task step short, and keep the close. Say what the group did not get to.

After the workshop

Note which task the group completed and where people got stuck; the next workshop starts from there. The platform link belongs in the meeting notes from now on; anyone new to the tools has the AI Basics there, seven lessons of three minutes.
