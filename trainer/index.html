Prepare your first session

Allow about an hour for preparation. Start with these three steps.

  1. Use Gemini in Google Sheets to summarise the example customer list.
  2. Choose a 30, 60 or 90 minute run sheet.
  3. Rehearse with a colleague for ten minutes.
