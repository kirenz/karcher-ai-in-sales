One continuous sales-cycle flow

The program is not a list of AI tricks. Its core, the course behind the third door, follows the Kärcher sales cycle as one continuous flow, from acquisition all the way to follow-up. The sales cycle itself is the structure, and each step’s output becomes the next step’s input.

flowchart TD
    A["1 · Acquisition"] --> B["2 · Research & Qualification"]
    B --> C["3 · First Contact"]
    C --> D["4 · Conversation Preparation"]
    D --> E["5 · Needs Analysis"]
    E --> F["6 · Value Argumentation"]
    F --> G["7 · Presentation & Demonstration"]
    G --> H["8 · Closing"]
    H --> I["9 · Follow-up & Steering"]

One fictional customer, NorthStar Facility Services Benelux, travels through every step — with its own website, a named buying center (Lars Meijer, Sophie Verbeek, Milan Bakker, Eva van Dijk), and a real product on the table (a pilot of three BR 45/22 scrubber driers, specs from the internal YellowFile). Because NorthStar is invented and cannot be researched, live research is demonstrated on ISS (issworld.com), a real, public facility-services company that stands in for a NorthStar-type account.

Deliberately light: closing and objection handling. It scored lowest in the needs survey (2.9). Step 8 covers it calmly (read the signal, classify the objection, no pressure); offer more only if a group asks.

Session sizes

Your live sessions realistically come in three sizes — plan in these, each backed by a ready session deck:

Size What fits inside Deck
One hour The Basics session as it is: tools, prompting, data gate, one card hands-on Basics session
Ninety minutes Basics, then a hands-on block: the cards the group’s answers put on top Basics session + cards
Two hours Basics compact, then two or three sales-cycle steps on NorthStar Basics session + Sprint (steps hidden)

Two smaller shapes exist around these: the team-meeting intro (15 minutes in an existing meeting, one card — creates demand; own deck ready) and card of the week (no session: one Quick-Help card per week, compared in the Monday call). A half or full day runs the whole cycle for a group that asks for it (the Sprint deck in full). Times are a budget, not a script.

Required accounts

Google Workspace with Gemini (Sheets, Docs, Slides, Gmail), Deep Research, Gems, and NotebookLM, signed in with the Kärcher account — the same sign-in unlocks the platform. Confirm participants can sign in before the session. For data exercises, the practice tracker is linked right on the pipeline card, so nobody needs to bring real data.
