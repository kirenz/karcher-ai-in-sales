Sales words

Pipeline, stage, segment and the other words in the workshop, in plain terms.

← The example workshop

For trainers who do not work in sales themselves: plain meanings of the words used in the workshop, the run sheet and the deck.

Pipeline
All the deals a seller is working on right now, seen together: which customer, what they might buy, how far the conversation has come, and roughly what it would be worth. A pipeline overview groups those deals so a manager sees where the money sits.
Practice tracker
Sales teams keep their pipeline in a list, often a spreadsheet, with one row per customer. The practice tracker is such a list, made up for the exercise: twelve fictional customer accounts, each with a stage, a segment, a value in euros and a note. Everyone works on their own copy, and nobody needs real customer data.
Stage
How far a deal has come: first contact, needs understood, offer sent, decision pending, won or lost. The tracker names the stage in one column.
Segment
The kind of customer, grouped by industry: Food & Beverage, Healthcare, Logistics and so on. Grouping by segment shows which industries carry the most value.
Report
A short written pipeline update in plain sentences, the kind a sales manager reads before a team meeting: the value in each stage, the biggest open deals, the customers that need attention.
Card
One page on the platform with one task: the instructions, the example data and the ready-written text for Gemini. The platform's quick-help area is a set of such cards.
Prompt
The text you paste into Gemini to tell it what you want. In this workshop the prompts are already written on the cards; participants copy them unchanged.
Ask Gemini
Gemini is Google's AI assistant. In Google Sheets it opens in a side panel called Ask Gemini; you paste the prompt there and it works on the open spreadsheet.
