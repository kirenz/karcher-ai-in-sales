Read it in your language

Everything here is written in English, but your browser translates every page in one click. And the AI answers in your language.

← Trainer hub

This works for you and for your participants, on this hub and on the learning platform. Two things are worth saying out loud in a session: the browser decides how it words a translated label, so a card can read slightly differently on their screen than on your slide, and the session scripts translate the same way, in one right-click.

Right-click anywhere on the page

In the menu that appears, choose Translate to Deutsch (or your own language). In Microsoft Edge it works the same way.

2

Not your language? Pick it once

Click the small translate icon in the address bar (top right), then ⋮ → Choose another language. Chrome remembers your choice.

3

The whole platform, in your language

Every page now reads in your language, automatically. Only the spoken Listen audio stays in English.

And the AI itself speaks your language

Translation is only half the answer. Write to Gemini and to the prepared Gems in your own language, and they answer in it: ask the sales coach for advice, practise a negotiation, draft a customer email for your market. The prompts on the cards are English, but you may reply, ask, and work in your language throughout. Only these pages and the spoken Listen audio are English.

On a phone: in the Chrome app, tap the ⋮ menu, then Translate.

Back to English: click the translate icon in the address bar, then Show original.
