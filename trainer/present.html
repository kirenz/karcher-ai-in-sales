Use the deck in Google Slides

Download the presentation file and open it in Google Slides using the steps below.

  1. Download the deck you need from the cards above.
  2. In Google Drive, choose New → File upload and select the downloaded file.
  3. Right-click the uploaded file and choose Open with → Google Slides. It opens as an editable presentation in your browser.
  4. Go through the slides and the speaker notes once, and rehearse before your session. Fix anything that sits oddly on your slides before you present.

Google's own instructions: uploading a presentation to Drive and opening it in Google Slides. To leave slides out of a run, select them in the thumbnail strip on the left, right-click and choose Skip Slide; the slides and their numbers stay, they are only skipped while you present, and clicking Skip Slide again brings them back (Google help).

Running a session in your language

The decks are written in English. Your participants do not have to wait for a translated edition, because three things already work in their language:

  • The platform reads in their language. One right-click in Chrome translates every page; the language guide shows how, and every page carries the globe icon that opens it.
  • The script pages translate the same way. Every deck has one, so you can prepare and present in your own words without a translated file.
  • The AI answers in their language. Gemini and the prepared Gems reply in whatever language they are written to, so coaching, negotiation practice, and customer emails all work locally.

One caveat worth saying out loud in the session: the browser decides how it words a translated label, so a card may read slightly differently on their screen than on your slide. Point at the position, and name the English original once.
