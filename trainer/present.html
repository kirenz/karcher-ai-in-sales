Running a session in your language

Your participants do not have to wait for a translated deck. Three things already work in their language:

  • The platform reads in their language. One right-click in Chrome translates every page; the language guide shows how, and every page carries the globe icon that opens it.
  • The AI answers in their language. Gemini and the prepared Gems reply in whatever language they are written to, so coaching, negotiation practice, and customer emails all work locally.
  • You speak your language. The slides are your script, not a teleprompter; present them in the room's language even while the screen shows English.

One caveat worth saying out loud in the session: the browser decides how it words a translated label, so a card may read slightly differently on their screen than on your slide. Point at the position, and name the English original once. Only the spoken audio tracks stay English.

Which size for which group? The four-week plan and the planning tools decide with you: one hour runs the Basics session as it is, ninety minutes adds your group's top cards, two hours adds sales-cycle steps from the Sprint.
