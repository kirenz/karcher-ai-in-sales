Get ready in three steps, about an hour

1 · Do both exercises yourself, about 25 minutes

New to Gemini yourself? Start with two AI Basics, Prompting and Data safety, three minutes each. Then do what your participants will do.

Exercise one, the pipeline table

  1. Open the card See your pipeline at a glance: instructions, the example spreadsheet and the prompt.
  2. Copy the practice tracker with File → Make a copy: twelve made-up accounts, no real customer data.
  3. In your copy, open Ask Gemini, paste the prompt unchanged, approve the plan if one shows. You get a table by stage and segment on a new tab, pipeline value.
  4. Check one total: Food & Beverage has two rows, 15,800 and 9,800, so the table must say 25,600. If it differs, look at which rows were included.

If the answer only appears in the side panel, ask again for the table on a new tab. If a button is missing or the copy fails, ask for access help before your workshop.

Exercise two, the account briefing (once, mainly to confirm the Gem opens for you)

  1. Open the Research lesson, Sales Cycle, step 2, and click through its five screens.
  2. On the exercise screen, copy the prompt (an account briefing for ISS, a real facility-services company) and click the black button: it opens the Account-Briefing Gem in Gemini, with your Kärcher account.
  3. Paste, start, approve the research plan if one shows, and let it run; a briefing takes a few minutes. Read it once.

If the Gem does not open with your Kärcher account, ask for access help before your workshop; participants will hit the same wall.

2 · Copy the deck and open the run sheet, about 10 minutes

Use the buttons at the top. Your deck copy opens in Google Slides; the run sheet follows its slide numbers. Paste the four links into the meeting invitation or keep them ready for the chat: the pipeline card, the practice tracker, the Research lesson, the Account-Briefing Gem. All four are on the run sheet.

3 · Read it through once, about 20 minutes

The script page from top to bottom, then the run sheet minute by minute. Note the one moment you want to say slowly. Two situations come up in almost every workshop:

The answer lands in the side panel, not on a new tab.

Say"Ask for the table specifically: put the result on a new tab called pipeline value. Then we compare one total with the source rows." If it stays in the panel: "Fine, we work with what you have. Take the numbers from the panel, and we do the check on those."

Someone says a total looks wrong.

Say"Good, show me. Which segment is it? Read me the values in those rows and we add them together here, out loud." Then: "If the summary shows the same figure, that segment matches. If it differs, we look at which rows it included and correct it."

Do not explain why the AI differed, and do not take the keyboard. Name the next action; the check settles it.

Before the workshop

  • Everyone can sign in to Google Workspace with the Kärcher account, use Ask Gemini in Sheets and open the Account-Briefing Gem.
  • Your copy of the deck opens in Google Slides.
  • Links ready to paste: the pipeline card, the practice tracker, the Research lesson, the Account-Briefing Gem.
  • On your own screen: the platform loads, the card opens, the tracker copies, the Gem opens. Screen sharing and sound work in the call.
  • At the end: the platform link goes into the meeting notes, and the invitation is "open the platform once this week and run one card on a list of your own".
