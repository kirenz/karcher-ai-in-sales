Prepare your session

Try the exercise, pick your length, copy the deck, read it through once. About an hour.

← Trainer Hub · Jump to run sheet and deck · other formats · adapt

A session is a 60-minute training you run with your sales team, from a prepared deck. In it, your colleagues open Google Sheets, ask Gemini one question about a practice customer list, and check the answer by hand. That is the exercise. It comes from the AI Sales Enablement platform, the learning site your teams already have, and we picked it because pipeline overviews were among the tasks that cost sales colleagues the most time in the survey. The platform holds more cards, from research to proposals to customer emails; the deck and the run sheet are built for this one, and the same pattern, card, prompt, check, works for any of them. Three steps get you ready, about an hour in total.

1 · Do the exercise yourself, about 15 minutes

Once, before the session, so you know what your participants will see.

New to Gemini yourself? Do two of the platform's AI Basics first: Prompting and Data safety, three minutes each.

  1. Open the exercise card See your pipeline at a glance. It holds the instructions, the link to the example spreadsheet and the prompt to paste into Gemini.
  2. Copy the practice tracker with File → Make a copy. Twelve fictional accounts; no customer data anywhere.
  3. In your copy, open Ask Gemini, paste the prompt unchanged, and approve the plan if Gemini shows one. You get a summary table by stage and segment on a new tab called pipeline value.
  4. Check one total. Add up one small segment in the original rows and compare it with the summary. If it differs, look at which rows were included.

If the answer only appears in the side panel, ask again for the table on a new tab. If a button is missing or the copy fails, ask for access help before your session.

3 · Read it through once, about 10 minutes

The run sheet minute by minute, then the script page for the slides you show. Note the one moment you want to say slowly. Two situations come up in almost every session:

The answer lands in the chat panel, not on a new tab.

Say"Ask for the table specifically: put the result on a new tab called pipeline value. Then we compare one total with the source rows." If it stays in the panel: "Fine, we work with what you have. Take the numbers from the panel, and we do the check on those."

Someone says a total looks wrong.

Say"Good, show me. Which segment is it? Read me the values in those rows and we add them together here, out loud." Then: "If the summary shows the same figure, that segment matches. If it differs, we look at which rows it included and correct it."

Do not explain why the AI differed, and do not take the keyboard. Name the next action; the check settles it.

Before participants arrive

  • Everyone can sign in to Google Workspace with the Kärcher account and use Ask Gemini in Sheets. Two people on one device is fine; plan time for both.
  • Your copy of the deck opens in Google Slides.
  • Links ready to paste: the pipeline card, the report card, the practice tracker.
  • In the room: the platform loads, one card opens, the tracker copies. Projector and sound work.
  • You know which activity to cut if time runs short; the run sheet says which.

After the session

  • End with the handoff: "now run the same recipe on one of your own lists."
  • Put the platform link in the meeting notes; it stays there from now on.
  • Next: the same session for the next group.

Adapt a session

Only when a group needs something different: very mixed experience, a market with its own priorities, a second session. Change how you explain and how you pair people; keep the task, the check and the minutes.
