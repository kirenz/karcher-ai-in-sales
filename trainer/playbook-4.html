Before the session

Where to get help

  • The content itself: the platform — the guide holds the depth on every card and course step.
  • Running a session: the three run sheets, the session scripts and the rehearsal sheet on the hub (Section 1).
  • Adapting one: Section 2 of this playbook; the pre-survey comes from Sales Enablement.
  • Sales Enablement / program owner: for accounts, the pre-survey form, and any client-specific compliance questions, contact your Kärcher Sales Enablement point of contact before your first session.

Seven days after the session

Ask which task participants repeated without the trainer, what they produced, and which source facts they checked. Record how many answered and how many reported checked independent reuse. Keep attendance and self-rated confidence as separate measures. Agree a named Kärcher contact for access and content questions before a wider rollout.
