When usage goes quiet

A dip after the start is normal. The routine brings it back.

← Trainer hub

Every rollout dips after the first weeks. The novelty wears off, a busy quarter lands, and the platform stops being opened. Expect it, plan for it, and treat it as a maintenance job, not a failure.

The routine that prevents most of it

Usage survives on rhythm, not enthusiasm. Three standing pieces, all inside the existing team meeting:

  • The link stays put. The platform link lives in the meeting notes, every week, forever.
  • The card of the week. You name one card; one use case, one habit. Rotate through what the survey ranked highest.
  • The one question. "What did you try, what did it save?" Thirty seconds per meeting keeps trying normal.

When it has already gone quiet

  • Restart small: a fifteen-minute refresher in the team meeting, one card, live. Not a new training.
  • Let a colleague show a win. Your survey names who already uses AI successfully; a peer demo beats a trainer demo.
  • Match the card to the season: pipeline and report cards before reviews and year-end, research cards before fair season, translation cards when campaigns localize.
  • Ask the thirty-day question honestly: "what did you actually use?" What comes back tells you which card to push next, and what to drop.

What not to do

Do not respond to a dip with a bigger training, and do not shame the team for not using it. Both raise the cost of coming back. The way back in is the same as the way in: one small card, inside a meeting that already exists.

If the quiet has a name, usually a leader who deprioritized it, go back to the pushback moves.
