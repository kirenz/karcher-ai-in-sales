Gemini@Production · learning hub

Two proposed ways to learn

Live durations include breaks. These options are for discussion and have not been agreed.

Choose a format

Compact · 180 minutes

One shared fictional case. Result: a group use-case draft and next-step checklist. No between-session assignment.

Transfer · 2 × 120 minutes

At least two weeks between sessions. Proposed supported preparation: 60–90 minutes. Result: a refined individual brief for review. Choose this if participants can prepare their own process.

Compact schedule · 180 minutes

First part · 80 min

Check-in 10; key terms 15; system/data map 25; international cases 20; break 10.

Second part · 100 min

Shared process lab 40 (25-minute sketch plus 15-minute assessment); approved Skill practice or offline rehearsal 25; Responsible Use 15; break 10; next steps 10.

Transfer · session 1 · 120 minutes

Build the foundation

Check-in 10; key terms 15; system/data map 25; international cases 20; break 10; shared lab 25; transfer briefing 15. The lab includes a four-minute two-question interview starter. No skill creation.

Transfer · session 2 · 120 minutes

Refine your own case

Transfer questions 10; own-case process refinement 25; idea assessment 25; break 10; approved Skill practice or offline rehearsal 25; Responsible Use 15; next steps 10.
