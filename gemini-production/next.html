Course outline

Build from one example.

The full training follows one fixed sequence. Each module ends with something you can use in your plant.

01

Find your case

Five signals point to candidates: repeated searches in several systems, paper or media breaks, waiting for information, lists kept twice, decisions without data. You leave with one candidate from your plant.

In the full training
02

Route the problem

Is it an SAP or MES topic, a fixed rule, a language-model step or an agent with tools? Name the existing-system alternative and who would operate the solution.

In the full training
03

Sketch current and future process

One-page template, three to four role lanes, five symbols. In the future process you mark the AI task, the rule checks and the human release.

In the full training
04

Check six criteria and hand over

Problem and trigger, routing, data and owners, benefit and risk, future process, next step. Score each 0, 1 or 2; from 9 of 12 the brief is ready for Kärcher’s use-case interview.

In the full training

The use-case interview targets a Workspace Skill; the existing approved Gem bridges the transition. Without tenant access, the training rehearses the first two interview questions offline.
