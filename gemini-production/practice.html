Try one sample: the document-change case

A fictional exercise you can try now. Read the packet, discuss three questions and open the worked checks. No live systems or operational instructions.

Goal · inputs · output · check

Goal

Reduce missed document impacts and duplicated searches across sites. Prepare an evidence-linked review package.

Inputs

Use only the invented A1, D1, M1, N1 and I1 records below. Snapshot: 2 November 2026, 09:00 Europe/Berlin.

Output

Current/future process; system/data map; triage and task split; up to four findings plus a short metadata note.

Check

Correct plant and variant, version, effective date, source authority, access and freshness. Name the specialist and release owner.

Fictional evidence packet

A1 · Approved change

CR17: variant V210 at plant P1 changes reference label Box-A to Box-B from 9 November 2026. Approved 2 November, 08:00. Owner: product-data steward. Labels specify no physical handling.

D1 · Controlled register

LR12 revision 4 is currently released and refers to Box-A. Review owner: logistics process specialist. No approved replacement is recorded. Owner: document controller.

M1 · MES export

V210/P1 links to LR12 revision 4. Extracted 30 October, 09:00. Owner: MES data owner. This is not evidence of today’s live assignment.

N1 · Unapproved note

A local coordinator asks whether the new reference could start on 6 November. Recorded 2 November, 08:30. There is no approval.

I1 · Idea catalogue

Idea C17 concerns document-revision impact alerts at plant P2. Coverage of V210/P1 and its data is unknown.

Exercise rules

MES extracts over 24 hours old need owner confirmation. An unapproved note cannot override an approved date. A future change does not automatically invalidate a currently released document.

Your 25-minute rehearsal

0–8 min · Read and sketch

Assign process owner, data steward and reviewer (3 min). Sketch current/future handovers and one repeated step to remove (5 min).

8–16 min · Map and draft

Map sources, owners, timestamps and unconfirmed connections (4 min). Apply SAP/register → rules → LLM → agent triage, then draft findings (4 min).

16–23 min · Review and rehearse

Peer-check evidence and escalation (3 min). Ask two interview questions: What repeated process problem changes? Which data and owners would be needed? Check possible reuse of C17 (4 min).

23–25 min · Share

Share one process change and one open prerequisite. A full interview, skill creation and a validated solution are outside this rehearsal.

Discuss first, then open the worked checks

Question 1

Can N1 move the effective date to 6 November?

Question 2

Does M1 confirm the current live document assignment?

Question 3

Can C17 be reused without checking its scope?

Worked checks · open after your discussion

Check 1 · Effective date

No. A1 is approved for 9 November; N1 is a question without approval. Preserve the conflict and refer it to the owner.

Check 2 · Current assignment

No. M1 is older than the fictional 24-hour threshold. Ask the MES owner to confirm today’s assignment.

Check 3 · Reuse

No. C17 may be a reuse candidate; its plant, variant, data and responsibilities still need checking.

Example metadata note · For expert review only

For variant V210 at plant P1, A1 schedules reference label Box-B from 9 November 2026. The impact on LR12 requires expert review. D1 records no approved replacement revision. Do not invent a revision or a physical handling step.
