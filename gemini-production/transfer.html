Prepare one process for review

Use an approved document for your answers. This local draft does not collect or send data.

A brief the next reviewer can assess

1 · Business problem

Describe the recurring process, affected roles/sites and intended business outcome. Avoid an office-efficiency-only framing.

2 · Current and future process

Sketch role-aware handovers, one repeated task to remove and the decision/release gates.

3 · Alternatives and reuse

Check existing SAP/MES/register capability, fixed rules and possible duplicates before proposing a language task or agent.

4 · Data and ownership

Name sources, identifiers, scope, version, authority, freshness, access and owners. Mark unknown connections.

5 · Measurement

Choose affected-reference accuracy, revision-package lead time, unresolved conflicts and total human effort. Define baseline, comparison period and owner. Include setup, operation and exception handling.

6 · Next action

Name reviewer, release owner and future operator/IT support. Record the next approval or information needed.

Optional fictional assessment · after the lab

Calculate total human time

Assume 12 cases per month across three sites in total. Current: 50 min preparation + 30 min review per case. Proposed: 25 + 30. Calculate before/after; then identify unmeasured work.

Worked check

Before: 960 min (16 h). Proposed: 660 min (11 h). Hypothesised difference: 300 min (5 h/month). Setup, data work, operation and exception rework remain unknown. This is a fictional hypothesis, not actual ROI.
